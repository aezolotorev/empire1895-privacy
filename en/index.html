Privacy Policy for Empire 1895: Tsar

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you play Empire 1895: Tsar (the “Game”) on Android (package com.empire1895.tsar) and in the web version on the Yandex Games platform, why it is processed, and what rights you have.

1. Who is responsible

The Game is developed by D one Games, an individual developer (“Developer”, “we”).
Contact for any privacy question: zolotorev1984@gmail.com.

2. What we do not collect

The Game has no registration or accounts. We do not request or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise (GPS) location or payment data.

3. What data is processed

The Game uses third-party analytics and advertising services. They automatically receive technical device data:

DataPurposeRecipient
Device identifiers: Advertising ID, Firebase installation IDanalytics, advertising, ad fraud preventionGoogle Firebase, Yandex Advertising Network
Approximate location (country / region derived from IP address)analytics, regional ad targetingGoogle Firebase, Yandex Advertising Network
In-game activity: launches, session length, game events (reign start and end, card choices), ad viewsanalytics and improving the Game, ad targetingGoogle Firebase, Yandex Advertising Network
Crash logs and diagnostics: device model, OS and Game version, language, load time, error detailsfinding and fixing bugsGoogle Firebase (Crashlytics)

Game progress (reigns, unlocked characters, artifacts, achievements, settings) is stored locally on your device. In the web version it may also be saved by the Yandex Games platform.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics) — Google LLC. Firebase privacy, Google Privacy Policy.
  • Yandex Advertising Network (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK) — YANDEX LLC. Yandex Privacy Policy, Yandex Mobile Ads SDK License.
  • Yandex Games (web version only) — the platform processes data under the Yandex Privacy Policy.

We do not sell data. Data is shared only with these services and only for the purposes listed. These services may process data on servers outside your country, including the USA and Russia.

5. Consent and how to withdraw it

  • On first launch the Game asks for consent to personalized ads and analytics. You can change your choice at any time in the Game settings.
  • If you decline, ads are shown without personalization and Firebase Analytics collection is turned off. Technical data required to serve ads and fix crashes (device identifier, crash logs) is still processed.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings: Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Uninstalling the Game deletes local progress and stops all data collection.

6. Retention and security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Retention is set by the services: user-level Firebase Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months, Crashlytics crash logs for about 90 days. Retention of Yandex Advertising Network data is described in the Yandex Privacy Policy.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take action.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR in the EU and UK, Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ and the CCPA/CPRA in California) you may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict processing, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we do not know your name or email, please include your device Advertising ID in your request. We reply within 30 days at zolotorev1984@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. By continuing to play after changes, you accept the updated policy.
